## Project-created artwork

House geometry, interface, procedural artwork, motion, and furniture logic were created for this project. Illustrated environment and furniture plates were generated using GPT Image and assembled, cleaned, and aligned in Aseprite. Editable masters and source plates are included in `art/house-themes/`. Runtime exports are in `public/assets/houses/`.

Project-created artwork is offered under the root MIT license to the extent the contributors hold applicable rights. This notice does not change the CC0 terms of the cats or the licenses of third-party dependencies. No image-generation service is required at runtime. System fonts are used.

Mewgenics was a design reference; no artwork, audio, binaries, or extracted game data from it is distributed here.
